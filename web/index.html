Methodology, sources and limitations

Evaluation

Held-out messages are stored in data/heldout_messages.csv; the result is generated by the reproducible evaluator and served from data/evaluation_results.json. The current version has 12 messages: 7 legitimate and 5 phishing.

The measured comparator is a TF-IDF-only score at a fixed 0.5 threshold on the same held-out split. It is a simple research baseline, not a production benchmark.
